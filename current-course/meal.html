Practical MEAL Training for NGO & Humanitarian Professionals

Build practical skills in Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning for development and humanitarian work.

3 monthsBDT 650Online • BanglaLive sessions

About the course

This practical course is designed for NGO and humanitarian professionals, project staff, MEAL/M&E practitioners, development-sector job seekers, students and fresh graduates who want to strengthen their applied MEAL skills.

What you can expect

  • Understand the core MEAL cycle and its role in project quality.
  • Explore monitoring tools, indicators and data collection workflows.
  • Strengthen accountability, feedback and learning practices.
  • Connect data quality and reporting with practical decisions.
Language: Bangla. Format: Online via Google Meet, with the published course schedule and details available on the registration platform.

Who should consider this course?

NGO staff, humanitarian practitioners, project officers, information management and MEAL team members, job seekers and learners interested in development-sector careers.
