Frequently asked

A few useful answers.

Are all listed courses open for registration?

No. Practical MEAL Training is the current featured course. Other course pages are clearly marked Coming Soon and are not accepting registrations yet.

Are courses online?

SCOPE is being presented as an online learning platform. Exact class format, schedule and access details will be stated on each course page when confirmed.

Will I receive a certificate?

Certificate availability depends on the specific course. For the current MEAL course, refer to its course information. SCOPE does not claim external accreditation unless explicitly stated.
