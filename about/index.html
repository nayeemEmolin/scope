About SCOPE

Education designed around real careers.

SCOPE stands for School for Career-Oriented Professional Education — an online learning initiative focused on practical skills for Bangladesh’s future professionals.

Our purpose

Help students, job holders and development practitioners build confidence with tools and methods they can apply in professional settings.

Our learning pathways span office productivity, Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning (MEAL), management information systems, data analytics and research methods.

“Sharpen your skills. Shape your future.”
